# App icon

The app uses `icon.png`: an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG generated with the built-in image generation tool, then resized for delivery. `project.godot` supplies it as the default application icon to the export presets. The former `icon.svg` is retained as an unused historical asset.

Approved September 7, 2026: a mint core gateway in a purple woodland clearing, surrounded by green pine trees, a winding parchment path, and curling slate-blue clouds. The ivory stone arch and mint heart form the focal point. This richer illustrated app artwork is an explicit exception to the minimal native gameplay art. The full-bleed square leaves the central gateway clear of platform corner masks. Do not bake rounded corners or transparency into the source.

An iOS rebuild and installation are required to update the home-screen icon. Desktop inspection does not verify an installed iOS build.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design a completely NEW square app icon for Hollow Vigil, an illustrated dark fantasy tower-defense game. Fresh composition from scratch, polished and confident. The focal point is the game's protected mint CORE GATEWAY, not a tall tower: an ancient squat hexagonal-topped ivory stone arch with a deep solid black recess, a bold floating mint diamond heart in the opening, a tiny ochre diamond crest above the arch, mint angled buttresses flanking the lower sides, and a low ivory stone plinth. Large simple polygon stone shapes with a few black seam marks, confident thick black outlines, original flat illustrated game-art style. Build a strong compact triangular woodland composition: the central arch occupies roughly 45 percent image width and 44 percent height, positioned slightly above center, two clusters of dark green triangular pine trees tucked BEHIND it to left and right, their tips completing the silhouette. A pale parchment trail curves elegantly from the lower right edge toward the central arch's base, narrowing only modestly; just one graceful sweeping bend, not a long busy S-road. Deep muted aubergine-purple ground everywhere, a few small grass marks and two angular stones. Wonderful layered curling slate-blue storm clouds enter from all four outer corners, with larger lower corner curls overlapping the lower path edges and smaller curls higher up, forming a loose atmospheric opening into the clearing, NO hard circular border. Compact iconic scene with careful overlap, excellent focal hierarchy and beautiful color balance: ivory and mint core against purple ground, forest-green pines, slate clouds, tiny ochre crest. The arch should feel substantial and magical, nestled into the woodland, no floating objects except the mint heart inside the arch. Rich but restrained flat 2D illustration, clean edges, screen-print-like solid color areas, hand-designed composition, no 3D, no realism, no gradients or neon glow, no blur. At phone icon size the core gateway must remain clearly readable. Preserve corner safe area for platform cropping; scenery runs full bleed to square edges. Opaque square app-icon artwork, 1024x1024. No text, lettering, logo words, mockup, rounded corners, frame, additional towers, enemies, or people. Aim for a mysterious welcoming hidden sanctuary rather than a generic castle emblem.
