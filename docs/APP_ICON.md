# App icon

The app uses `icon.png`: an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG generated with the built-in image generation tool, then resized for delivery. `project.godot` supplies it as the default application icon to the export presets. The former `icon.svg` is retained as an unused historical asset.

The design draws on the Ashneedle tower silhouette, mint core identity, parchment foundations, and forest terrain. The enemy lineup informed the bold black outlines and simple shapes. A full-bleed green background replaces the old inset border; the central tower leaves the corners clear for platform masking. Do not bake rounded corners or transparency into the source.

An iOS rebuild and installation are required to update the home-screen icon. Desktop inspection does not verify an installed iOS build.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create the finished square app icon for Hollow Vigil, a minimal illustrated dark fantasy tower defense game. References show the actual game's enemies, towers and tiles; match their elementary flat polygon shapes, solid thick black outlines and restrained palette, not their sheet layouts or text. One large centered ochre watchtower, triangular pointed roof and modest crown-like side battlements inspired by the Ashneedle upgraded tower. Parchment plinth, a bold black arched opening containing a simple mint diamond representing the defended core. Strong instantly readable silhouette. Flat forest green #95AA83 background extending fully to all four square edges. Use ochre #E0B568, parchment #E8DDBD, mint #93C9BC and black #000000. Subject entirely within central 70 percent of canvas, with generous safe space at corners. Tower occupies about 68 percent height and 58 percent width. Very clean balanced emblem, few large shapes, exact solid fills, confident heavy black strokes. No outer border, no frame, no rounded canvas corners, no inset tile, no text, no additional enemies or landscape, no gradient, no lighting, no shadows, no texture, no 3D or bevel. Fully opaque 1024x1024 square production icon, not a mockup.
